# CSXXL factory patches

768 slots = 64 written recipes (8 families x 8) x 12 fixed variations. Slot n: family = n mod 8, recipe = (n/8) mod 8, variation = n/64 (0..11). Names in the plugin stay numbers.

## BRASS
1. Blade brass: slow-swelling saw+pulse, filter opens after the key, aftertouch bite
2. Fanfare: fast attack, bright
3. Soft horn: square, dark and round
4. Sforzando: hard bite that settles
5. Trombone: mono, slide, slow attack
6. Trumpet: pulse-heavy, resonant, vibrato late
7. Brass section: two lines, one wide and bright
8. Octave brass: octaves wave, big and hollow

## STRINGS
1. Warm strings: slow, wide, soft filter
2. Bright strings: high-pass thin top
3. Slow swell strings
4. Pizzicato: short, plucked, no sustain
5. Solo violin: mono, vibrato by aftertouch
6. Cello-like: low, dark, round
7. String machine: ramp plus pulse, chorus
8. Tremolo strings: LFO on amplitude

## PAD
1. Tears pad: slow filter, soft attack, big release
2. Glass pad: high-passed, resonant shimmer
3. Dark choir-ish pad: square+pulse, low filter
4. Warm wash: no filter movement, very slow
5. Moving pad: LFO on the filter
6. Sweeping pad: filter attack long
7. Sine bed pad: sub sine under a soft saw
8. Pulse-width pad: wide PWM motion

## BASS
1. Sub bass: sine + sub, short filter
2. Saw bass: bright pluck
3. Square bass: round
4. Resonant acid-ish bass: high resonance, fast env
5. Fat unison bass: two lines, detuned
6. Pulse bass: PWM
7. Slide bass: long portamento
8. Organ-ish bass: peak wave, no filter env

## LEAD
1. Deckard lead: saw+pulse, mono, ribbon-friendly vibrato
2. Whistle lead: sine-heavy, soft
3. Square lead: hollow
4. Screamer: high resonance, hard filter env
5. Soft flute lead: breath noise in the attack
6. Sync-ish lead: octaves wave
7. Slow-vibrato lead: LFO fades in
8. Dual lead: line B an octave-ish detune up

## ENSEMBLE
1. Brass over strings: A strings, B brass
2. Strings and choir pad
3. Split-brass ensemble
4. Organ and strings
5. Pad and bell
6. Wide brass choir
7. Strings with airy noise
8. Layered pad and lead

## FX
1. Wind: white noise, resonant band sweeping
2. Surf: noise with slow swell and fall
3. Whoosh: fast noise sweep
4. Laser: pitch-ish blip via filter, ring mod
5. Dark drone: low square pair, slow movement
6. Sample and hold: random filter steps
7. Bit-crushed texture: noise plus crush
8. Chime FX: ring modulated bell

## ARP
1. Up arp: 1/8 saw pluck
2. Up-down arp: bright, 2 octaves
3. Down arp: dark pluck
4. Fast arp: 1/16
5. Echo arp: arp plus tempo echo
6. Hold arp: latched chord with pad underneath
7. Keys and arp: A plays keys, B arpeggiates (use keysplit)
8. Random-ish arp: up-and-down 2

## Variations (applied to every recipe)
0. as written
1. brighter
2. darker
3. slower attack and release
4. short and plucked
5. wide: detune, chorus, more width
6. echo and reverb
7. expression: vibrato from the LFO, aftertouch on the filter
8. sub octave under line B
9. noise breath layer on line A
10. mono with glide
11. ring mod / crush colour
